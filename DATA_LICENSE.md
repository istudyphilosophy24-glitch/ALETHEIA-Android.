# Dictionary data attributions

PHILOSOPHIA's small technical philosophy seed contains newly authored short explanations.

Large packs are compiled from the English Wiktionary word and sense records extracted by Wiktextract and distributed by Kaikki.org (Tatu Ylonen; original contributors to Wiktionary). Wiktionary content is made available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike and GNU Free Documentation License as applicable. Preserve the original attributions, share-alike obligations and full appropriate license terms if distributing a derived database. Source: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/ and https://www.wiktionary.org/ .

The GitHub Actions workflow builds the data from live source material; PHILOSOPHIA never fabricates counts.
